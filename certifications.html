QHSE Commitment

Three pillars of assured delivery.

Safety First

Health and safety management precedes all activity — embedded in planning, execution and shutdown works through a genuinely EHSQ-conscious culture.

Quality Assurance

A robust QA/QC system governs WPS/PQR control, NDT, dimensional and hydrostatic testing, and third-party / client inspection across shop and site.

Environment

ISO 14001-certified environmental management minimises impact across fabrication, painting and construction operations.

Policy

An integrated QHSE management system.

Cylingas' QHSE management systems are certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001. As a holder of the ASME 'U' and 'U2' stamps and the NBBI 'R' stamp, every fabricated and repaired item is produced under documented, auditable procedures — supported by certified inspectors and a complete NDT capability.
