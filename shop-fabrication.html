Capability · Fabrication

Shop Fabrication — ASME-coded, to 70 mm plate.

Design, engineering, procurement, fabrication, painting and testing of ASME-coded process equipment in our 300,000 sq ft Dubai facility.

Our fabrication shop produces pressure vessels and columns carrying the ASME 'U' and 'U2' stamp, above- and underground storage vessels, rectangular tanks, atmospheric vessels, silos, floating suctions and skid-mounted units for a wide range of process applications — supported by certified welding procedures and a robust QA system.

SHOP FABRICATION

Fabrication Equipment

  • Hydraulic plate rolling to 70 mm thick
  • CNC cutting / plasma, 3,600 mm rail gauge
  • CNC drilling to 100 mm plate thickness
  • Dedicated scaffolding team & tooling
  • NACE / BGAS-supervised painting
  • SMAW · FCAW · TIG · SAW welding

Products Fabricated

  • Pressure vessels & atmospheric tanks
  • Heat exchangers & LPG storage tanks
  • Air receivers, separators & scrubbers
  • Silos, strainers & surge tanks
  • Process & LPG skids
  • Waste-gas knock-out drums

Quality & Testing

  • ASME Sec. VIII Div. 1 & 2 compliance
  • NBBI 'R' stamp repair capability
  • Radiographic & ultrasonic testing
  • PT / MPI & hydrostatic testing
  • Certified welders & WPS/PQR control
  • Third-party & client inspection
