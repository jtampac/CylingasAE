The Company

A foundation built on five decades of industrial delivery.

Cylingas Company L.L.C. was established on 28 April 1974 by decree of the late ruler of Dubai, His Highness Sheikh Rashid Bin Saeed Al Maktoum, and began operations in 1976 as the first GCC company in Dubai to manufacture gas cylinders and pressure vessels.

From those origins the company evolved into a full EPC and fabrication contractor — turning to bulk liquid storage as the region's Oil & Gas sector expanded, and today delivering complete storage facilities, process equipment and infrastructure for the UAE's most demanding operators.

  • Prequalified ADNOC EPC / Fabrication contractor
  • ASME U, U2 & NBBI R stamp holder
  • ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 certified
  • UAE, GCC & international delivery
