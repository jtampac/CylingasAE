Capability · EPC

EPC Projects — engineered, procured, constructed.

Design, engineering, project management, construction and commissioning of complete storage and process facilities across the Oil & Gas, energy and utility sectors.

Cylingas delivers full EPC scopes — from spheres, vertical storage tanks and tank foundations to plant piping, structural, mechanical, civil and electrical & instrumentation works for process plants, oil storage terminals, airports and power plants. We construct and erect process equipment including pressure vessels, columns, towers, flare stacks, bullets, pig launcher/receivers and marine loading arms, alongside thermal energy storage (TES) tanks.
