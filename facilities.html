Fabrication Workshop

A heavy-fabrication hall equipped for pressure vessels, columns, tanks and skids — under one QA system from cut to paint.

Plate Rolling

Hydraulic plate-rolling capability to 70 mm, forming heavy shells for vessels, spheres and large-diameter tanks.

Welding Facilities

Certified welding across SMAW, FCAW, TIG and SAW, executed by qualified welders to controlled procedures.

Dimensional, hydrostatic and NDT inspection — RT, UT, PT and MPI — supporting third-party and client witness points.

Painting under NACE / BGAS supervision, pickling and passivation, and full pre-delivery testing and certification.
